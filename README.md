关于GPL-3
    GPL-3（GNU通用公共许可证第3版）是一款具有“强传染性”的自由软件许可证，其核心在于确保软件自由在未来所有衍生作品中得以延续。只要你将GPL-3协议的代码纳入自己的项目，并与之结合形成 “一个衍生作品” 进行发布，那么整个衍生作品都必须以GPL-3协议开源。如果仅仅是和你的专有程序打包在一起（聚合），或通过进程间通信等方式独立协作，通常不会触发传染。判断标准在于代码是否深度融合、相互依赖。当你对外分发GPL-3程序的任何二进制形式（如可执行文件）时，开源义务即被触发。必须同时向接收者提供完整的、人类可读的源代码。可以通过随软件附带、提供有效的下载链接或书面要约等方式实现。在司法实践中（包括中国），GPL系列许可证被认定为具有法律约束力的著作权许可合同。使用即表示接受条款。违反协议会导致授权自动终止。但协议也提供了补救和恢复机制（如停止侵权后，权利可能恢复）。允许商用，例如销售包含GPL-3软件的产品、提供技术服务或支持。若想将你的专有代码与之结合并闭源销售，在技术上必须确保两者是独立的（如聚合体），否则整个产品都需开源。贡献者自动授予用户实施其专利的许可。如果你对该程序发起专利侵权诉讼，则依据GPL-3获得的所有权利（包括专利许可）将被自动终止。如果你仅通过网络提供软件服务（SaaS），而不对外分发软件本身，传统的GPL-3可能不强制要求开源你的后端代码。对此，GNU设计了AGPL协议，它明确将SaaS视为“分发”，要求开源服务端代码。许多云服务商采用AGPL来保护其开源成果。
About GPL-3
    GPL-3 (GNU General Public License Version 3) is a free software license with a strong "copyleft" nature. Its core principle is to ensure that software freedom is preserved in all future derivative works. As long as you incorporate code under the GPL-3 license into your own project and combine it to create "a derivative work" for distribution, the entire derivative work must be open-sourced under the GPL-3 license. If it is merely bundled with your proprietary program (as an aggregation) or interacts through independent collaboration like inter-process communication, the copyleft effect is typically not triggered. The key criterion is whether the codes are deeply integrated and interdependent.When you distribute any binary form of a GPL-3 program (e.g., an executable file), the open-source obligation is triggered. You must simultaneously provide recipients with complete, human-readable source code. This can be achieved by including it with the software, providing a valid download link, or a written offer.In legal practice (including in China), the GPL series of licenses are recognized as legally binding copyright licensing contracts. Using the software implies acceptance of its terms. Violating the license will result in automatic termination of rights. However, the license also provides mechanisms for remediation and reinstatement (e.g., rights may be restored after ceasing infringement).Commercial use is permitted, such as selling products containing GPL-3 software or providing technical services and support. If you wish to combine your proprietary code with it and sell it as closed-source, you must technically ensure they are independent (e.g., an aggregation); otherwise, the entire product must be open-sourced.Contributors automatically grant users a license to practice their patents. If you initiate a patent infringement lawsuit against the program, all rights obtained under GPL-3 (including the patent license) will be automatically terminated.If you only provide software services over a network (SaaS) without distributing the software itself, the traditional GPL-3 may not compel you to open-source your backend code. To address this, GNU designed the AGPL license, which explicitly treats SaaS as "distribution" and requires the server-side code to be open-sourced. Many cloud service providers adopt AGPL to protect their open-sources.
